Senior Switch Playbook

A 12-week plan that gets you ready for senior backend interviews at high-paying companies and moves you toward AI engineering at the same time. Every item in the plan links to a lesson on this site. The question bank comes from about 300 interview reports posted between late 2024 and October 2026, at more than 45 companies.

AI is now inside the interviewRazorpay, Flipkart, Amazon (in the OA), LinkedIn, Microsoft, Atlassian, Rippling, Stripe and Postman run coding rounds where you use an AI agent. They grade your design, your prompts, how you review its diffs, and whether your tests pass.
Machine coding beats LeetCode at Indian product companiesExpect 90 minutes building a booking system, rate limiter or scheduler that actually runs, followed by a review of your code covering concurrency and design patterns.
The project deep-dive rejects peopleBlinkit, CRED, Slice and Freshworks rejected candidates in the hiring-manager round. Prepare to explain what you built, separate from the team, and why you chose each piece of tech.
Downleveling is commonAmazon SDE2→SDE1, JPMC VP→SE3, Atlassian P50→P40. Prepare system design properly so you land at senior level.

Pace: about 10 hours a week. The left column prepares you for the switch; the right column builds the AI engineering skills. Start applying in week 9 and use weeks 11–12 for mock interviews while interviews are running.

Everything you need to learn is on these pages: concepts explained from scratch, Java solutions for every listed problem, full design walkthroughs, and the follow-up questions interviewers asked in recent reports. You'll only need LeetCode to submit solutions.

These are real offers people posted (mostly on LeetCode Discuss), Sep 2024 – Oct 2026, India. "Year 1" includes base, bonus, sign-on and first-year stock. ESOPs at private companies are illiquid, so compare base first.
